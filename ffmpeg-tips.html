FFMPEG Tips

TechnologyHowTolearning

FFMPEG, affectionately known as the Swiss Army knife of video editing tools, is an indispensable command-line based tool for every video editor's toolkit. Sometimes, you encounter a video that refuses to play. Fear not, for with FFMpeg, you have a high chance of transforming that unusable file into one that is playable.

If you're looking for tips and tricks on how to utilize FFMpeg effectively, there's a website that has you covered. Simply copy and paste the commands and process the files as needed.

https://ffmpegbyexample.com/
